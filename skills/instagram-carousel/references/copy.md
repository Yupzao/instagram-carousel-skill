# Copy: hooks, slide text, CTA and caption

Write the copy in the user's language (default). The formulas below are in English; adapt them to the language and the niche.

## Cover hooks
Pick the formula that fits the content:
- **Contrast**: "Noob vs Pro [topic]" · "Ugly vs Viral" · "Amateur vs Professional"
- **Paradox / thesis**: "Same information. One gets ignored, one goes viral."
- **Personal proof**: "I used [tool] to make these [results]"
- **Number + promise**: "7 mistakes that make your [X] invisible"
- **Direct stop**: "Stop using [common thing]" (with the alternative on the following slides)
- **Identity**: "If you're a [audience], save this"
- **Effortless result**: "[Result] in [short time], without [pain]"

Rules: title ≤ 6 words, subtitle ≤ 12. The title talks about the **result**, not the topic
("About the topic. Sounds boring." ✗ / "About the result. Makes you curious." ✓).

## Text inside the slides
- Title of 1–4 words (the concept) and supporting text ≤ 15 words.
- Short paired labels: "Generic & boring" / "Striking & curiosity-driven".
- Imperative verbs, second person.
- Big numbers when there are any (10G, 5G, 59.6K): they act as visual anchors.
- Highlight 1–2 words per slide (bold, pop colour or colour box). Never more.

## Last-slide CTA
Only one. In order of strength:
1. **Comment a word → reward**: "Comment 'GUIDE' and I'll send you the [concrete resource with a number]."
2. **Save**: "Save this for when you need it." (with a bookmark icon)
3. **Share**: "Send this to someone who needs to see it."
4. **Follow**: "Follow @handle for more [topic]."

## Caption
Structure (adapt the length to the archetype: A = long, B/C/D = 1–3 lines):

```
[1-line hook with 1 emoji, different from the cover title]

[2–3 lines of context/value that do NOT repeat the slides]

💬 Comment "[WORD]" and [reward].
📌 Save this for later.
📤 Share it with someone who [audience].
❤️ Follow @handle for [promise].

#hashtag1 #hashtag2 #hashtag3 #hashtag4 #hashtag5

Keywords:
[keyword 1]
[keyword 2]
…
```

- 3–5 niche-specific hashtags (not 30 generic ones).
- Plain-text keywords help Instagram search. Use 4–8 terms the audience would search for.
- Archetype C: 1 brand sentence + 2–3 own hashtags.
- Archetype D: 1 line of context + emoji.
